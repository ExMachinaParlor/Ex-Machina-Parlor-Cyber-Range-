Ask the EMP range crew

  1. Confirm this range is running and choose an exercise with the crew.
  2. Get your assigned workstation or connection method, account, and target scope.
  3. Review the event rules, available time, and how to request help or a reset.
  4. Record what you tried, what changed, and the evidence you observed.

Access and event details are supplied on site. Use only the systems and activities assigned for your session; coordinate changes and resets with the crew.

Your session

Ask the crew which range-day configuration is running. The public archive describes EMP-FAK4, but does not establish the current conference setup.

Suggested starting exercise

Review the event topology with the crew, identify your assigned systems, and follow the exercise selected for this session.

What to take away

Document your route through the exercise, the evidence you collected, and any questions for the walkthrough.
