Access and event details are supplied on site. Use only the systems and activities assigned for your session; coordinate changes and resets with the crew.

Your session

Ask the crew whether the physical robotics arena is running and how teams are assigned. The documented format pairs a pilot with a hacker.

Suggested starting exercise

Review the arena and approved robot with the crew. Agree on pilot and hacker roles, then practice the event objective within the assigned rules.

What to take away

Connect robot movement, hardware interfaces, and wireless security with teamwork and a physical objective. Follow the crew’s current arena and RF rules.

The arena

The documented DEFCON Hack the Bots format places four teams in an obstacle-filled arena. A pilot drives the robot while a hacker works on the permitted wireless, IoT, and hardware challenges.

The objective

Reach the central platform, capture the physical flag, and defend the position. The arena design includes rotary and piston obstacles plus IoT-controlled barriers.

Rules and participation

Read the repository rules and event-specific RF requirements before participating. The rules prohibit permanent robot damage. The repository lists info@exmachinaparlor.net for participation questions; confirm current event availability with the organizers.
